Bar Chat Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 14, 2026

Bar Chat lets you see which bars your friends are checked in at, and lets bars share tonight's staff and specials. This policy explains what we collect, why, and the choices you have. Bar Chat is for people 21 and older.

What we collect

  • Account: when you use Sign in with Apple, we receive an Apple account identifier and, if you choose to share them, your name and an email address (which may be an Apple private relay address).
  • Profile: the display name you choose and your invite code.
  • Friends: your friend requests, friends, close friends, and the people you hide your check-ins from.
  • Check-ins: the bar you check in at, when it starts and ends, and who can see it (all friends, close friends, or just you).
  • Settings: ghost mode, default audience and how long check-ins last.
  • Bar accounts: for bar owners and staff, the bar you manage, staff invites and the specials you post.

Location

With your permission, Bar Chat uses your phone's location for two things:

  • To show bars near you. Your phone asks Apple Maps for nearby bars; we do not store your location for this.
  • To confirm you are at a bar when you check in. Your location is sent once to our server to check that you are within about 150 meters of the bar, and is then discarded. We never store your exact location, and friends only ever see the bar's name.

You can turn location off at any time in your iPhone's Settings. You will still be able to browse bars.

Who can see your information

  • Your friends see your display name. They see your check-in only if its audience includes them, you are not in ghost mode, and you have not hidden your check-ins from them.
  • Check-ins stop being visible to friends when they end, either when you tap Leave or automatically after 2, 3 or 5 hours.
  • People who are not your friends cannot see your check-ins.
  • Bar staff names are shown on a bar's page only after the staff member accepts their invite.

How we use it

Only to run Bar Chat: signing you in, showing friends where they are, and showing bars' staff and specials. We do not sell your information, show ads, or track you across other apps or websites.

Service providers

  • Supabase hosts our database and sign-in.
  • Apple provides Sign in with Apple and Apple Maps search results.

Keeping and deleting your data

We keep your information while you have an account. To delete your account and everything linked to it (profile, friends, check-ins and settings), email us at the address below and we will do it within 30 days. You can also remove friends, unhide people and leave a check-in at any time in the app.

Children

Bar Chat is not for anyone under 21, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date above and, for significant changes, let you know in the app.
